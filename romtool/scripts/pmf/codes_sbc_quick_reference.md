# ERCES code matrix, SBC Quick Code Reference (retrieved 2026-11-26)

Source: Safer Buildings Coalition Quick Code Reference, https://saferbuildings.org/code-reference/
(produced by SBC; draws on Chapter 5 of the Complete ERCES Handbook with the NICET IB-PSC Study Guide, by
Perdue, Foley, Brownson). Side by side across IFC 2015/2018/2021/2024 and NFPA 72-2013 /
NFPA 1221-2016/2019 / NFPA 1225-2022. Use as the requirement map for the public safety phase; before a
ROM relies on a section, re-read the primary text at codes.iccsafe.org (IFC, public) or codes.nfpa.org
(FNPA, free access with an account). Key tool-facing values: coverage 95 percent general and 99 percent
critical with DAQ 3.0 (NFPA 1225 18.8 and 18.9; IFC 510.4.1), -95 dBm is IFC 2015/2021-only and not in
the IFC 2024 or NFPA 1225 text, UL 2524 listing (IFC 510.4 from 2021; NFPA 1225 18.12.1.3), pathway
survivability per 18.12.3 with no backbone fire rating in fully sprinklered buildings (18.12.3.3/4),
plenum/riser cable per 18.12.3.1, lightning protection per 18.4 (NFPA 70 Art. 820, UL 497C antennas),
noise floor 510.4.2.9 / 18.9.3, standby power 12 h battery or generator (IFC 510.4.2.3; 1225 18.13.2,
2 h battery plus generator alternative), donor isolation 20 dB above system gain (510.4.2.4(4);
1225 18.3.3.2 and 18.10), oscillation prevention 18.3.2, monitoring per fire alarm 18.14 with the
annunciator where a fire command center exists (18.14.2.1), AHJ approval and personnel qualifications
(18.2, 3.3.115), tests initial acceptance annual and 5-year (18.5, 20.3.10.1), FCC 90.219 compliance
(510.5.5, 18.7.2), antenna density and near-far (18.3.4), donor mounting (18.3.3), design documents
(18.7.1), technical criteria by AHJ (18.15).
